Hi, I'm Miki, a game programmer from Croatia by day and night.
I started game development in Flash AS3, have limited experience with Unreal and Godot, and use Unity on a daily basis.

Game programming has been my passion for many years now.
I like exploring new features in engines, gpu programming, physics, and optimization problems, but my biggest passion is code architecture.

I hold a masters in computer science since 2020. and have been working professionally in the games industry since 2016. having gained experience in large and small projects, mobile and console porting, organization, team leadership, project planning and small team management.

In hopes of sharing what I've learnt and will learn, I decided to make this site. Hope you find something useful :)
